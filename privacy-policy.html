Paw Blocks

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Paw Blocks ("the App") is a block-placing game. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the App and what privacy choices you have.

Data controller and contact

App developer: Sezer Kuşku
Contact: sezerkusku3@gmail.com

Account and data stored on your device

The App does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your best scores, chosen animals, sound and vibration preferences, whether you finished the tutorial, and the in-app status of the "Ad-free" purchase are stored on your device. These game preferences are not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the App deletes its data from your device; your operating system's backup and restore features may also apply.

Ads

The App may show ads through AdMob, a service of Google LLC. AdMob may process data such as the advertising identifier (IDFA or Android Advertising ID, where permitted), IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions in order to serve ads, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud and measure performance. Google may process this data on servers outside your country. Details: Google advertising technologies.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the required ad consent is requested the first time you open the App. Where required, you can change your choice under "Privacy options" on the Settings screen. On iOS, if you don't grant Apple's tracking permission, the App does not access the IDFA; ads may still be shown. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

The "Ad-free" purchase is made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your payment details never reach the developer. The store passes the App the transaction information needed to complete and restore the purchase.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13.

Your rights and requests

For requests under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK, Turkish Personal Data Protection Law) and for any privacy questions, write to sezerkusku3@gmail.com. Requests about advertising data can also be sent to Google.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the App and at the link on the store page.
